# The Best New Holiday Romance Books of 2026, by Trope

Holiday romance books make a good December reading list when you pick by mood. For a snowed-in story, start with Lauren Blakely’s _Mistletoe All the Way_. If you want a Christmas fake engagement, try Meghan Quinn’s _Christmas Staycation_. Amanda Elliot’s _Eight Ways to Say I Love You_ follows two childhood friends across eight years of Hanukkah celebrations.

The books below are U.S. releases from the 2026 holiday season. ## Snowed in

### Mistletoe All the Way by Lauren Blakely

A holiday visit to a snowy town brings a fake relationship, twin confusion and plenty of chances for the couple’s rehearsed affection to become real. Choose it if you like the pace and warmth of a small-town Christmas rom-com and want more heat than a TV holiday movie usually offers. The publisher’s listing includes reviews describing it as a spicy read. [Atria Books, October 6](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Mistletoe-All-the-Way/Lauren-Blakely/9781668233795).

### ’Tis the Damn Season by Rachel Lacey

Two women end up stranded at a Vermont house when a fallen tree blocks the driveway and a storm is on the way. It is a good pick if you like a small setting where the characters finally have time to deal with the thing they have been avoiding. Put it on your list for a sapphic holiday romance with the weather doing a lot of the matchmaking. [Rachel Lacey’s title page, November 10](https://rachellacey.com/books/tis-the-damn-season/).

## Fake dating and accidental relationships

### Christmas Staycation by Meghan Quinn

Taran has to keep a fake engagement credible in front of her family, and the man she actually starts falling for is her ex’s brother. The prospect of a quiet Christmas falls away quickly. Pick this one if you want the humor to come from an increasingly indefensible story, with family pressure and a relationship that gets more interesting every time explaining it would get harder. [Bloom Books, October 20](https://www.sourcebooks.com/9781464243684-christmas-staycation-deluxe-edition-tp.html).

### The Christmas Getaway by Debbie Mason

Charlotte heads to Scotland with a story about a Scottish boyfriend that she now needs to keep up. On the flight, she befriends a girl who is about to move in with her father, Ewan, a duke. Try it if you like a holiday romance whose best complication comes from the people the heroine meets when her carefully arranged trip gets going. [Forever, October 13](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/debbie-mason/the-christmas-getaway/9781538778081/).

## Forced proximity with a mystery

### The Santa Claus Con by Lauren Kung Jessen

FBI agents Scarlett and Nathan need to work together again after a threat against a New York holiday event. They already know they dislike Christmas, and they have a messier history with each other than either wants to address on the job. Choose this for a bit of suspense between the holiday scenes and an old romance that has a reason to flare up quickly. [Forever, October 27](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/lauren-kung-jessen/the-santa-claus-con/9781538779071/).

## Second chances and friends-to-lovers

### Eight Ways to Say I Love You by Amanda Elliot

Shira comes home to New Jersey for Hanukkah with a new job and life in Los Angeles. Her childhood friend Leo has had a tougher year. Over the next eight years, family celebrations bring their lives into view again as they work out what they want. Pick it for a friendship that has time to change and a holiday that means something beyond the scenery. [Berkley, October 27](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/808613/eight-ways-to-say-i-love-you-by-amanda-elliot/).

### The Holiday House Swap by Lauren Appelbaum

Phoebe and Arden switch homes to get away from their own holiday plans. One finds herself invited to a Hanukkah celebration with her temporary host’s brother. The other runs into someone whose heart she broke in college. This is a good choice when you want Christmas and Hanukkah in the same story, or when a single romantic pairing feels too tidy. [Hachette, October 20](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/lauren-appelbaum/the-holiday-house-swap/9781538784518/).

## Short stories and novella collections

### A Married Little Christmas by Abby Jimenez

Three novellas, recipes and illustrations give this collection the feel of a holiday gift you could read in installments. One setup reconnects high-school exes at a family dinner, where a fake date helps them get through the evening. Another puts a reluctant costumed Santa in a diner with a server he might actually like. [Forever, October 20](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/abby-jimenez/a-married-little-christmas/9781538780060/).

### The Twelve Loves of Christmas by Alexandria Bellefleur and eleven other authors

The anthology is laid out as an Advent-style collection of twelve separate romances. Try one story per evening or keep it on hand for pockets of time over the holidays. The contributors include Elise Bryant, Olivia Dade, Alexis Daria, M. A. Wardell and Rebekah Weatherspoon. [Atria Books, September 29](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Twelve-Loves-of-Christmas/Alexandria-Bellefleur/9781668250419).

### Snow This Is Love by Christina Lauren, Jasmine Guillory, Julie Murphy, Zoraida Córdova and Jesse Q. Sutanto

Five authors bring a Disney-inspired angle to a collection of modern holiday romances. It suits a reader who likes familiar story shapes and wants a different take from one sitting to the next. [Hyperion Avenue, October 13](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/826755/snow-this-is-love-by-christina-lauren-jasmine-guillory-jesse-q-sutanto-julie-murphy-zoraida-cordova/9781368118064/).

## Choose your heat level and pair it with a movie

The publisher descriptions make _Mistletoe All the Way_ and _Christmas Staycation_ good starting points if you want heat alongside the holiday comedy. Check the current publisher descriptions or sample pages when you are choosing a gift for someone with a firm preference. Trope and heat describe different things: a fake engagement is a setup, and the author can write it with any level of on-page detail.

For a book and movie night, choose the thread you want to carry over. Follow an old-flame story with another second-chance romance or match a festive mystery with a film that gives you plenty to guess at. ## Frequently asked questions

### What are some new holiday romance books for 2026?

Try _Mistletoe All the Way_ for a snowed-in setup, _Christmas Staycation_ for fake dating or _Eight Ways to Say I Love You_ for Hanukkah and childhood friends. All three have U.S. fall publication dates.

### Is there a new Hanukkah romance?

Yes. Amanda Elliot’s _Eight Ways to Say I Love You_ follows childhood friends across eight years. Lauren Appelbaum’s _The Holiday House Swap_ also has a Hanukkah storyline alongside its Christmas setting.

### What is a good fake-dating Christmas romance?

Meghan Quinn’s _Christmas Staycation_ features a fake engagement with family complications and a growing attraction to an ex’s brother. Debbie Mason’s _The Christmas Getaway_ uses a pretend boyfriend and a trip to Scotland.

### What should I read if I want something short?

Choose one of the three novellas in _A Married Little Christmas_ or one of the twelve stories in _The Twelve Loves of Christmas_. _Snow This Is Love_ is another collection you can read in several sittings.

### Which holiday romances are spicy?

Publisher descriptions identify _Mistletoe All the Way_ and _Christmas Staycation_ as good options for a spicier read. Check sample pages or publisher notes for other titles if heat level matters to you.
