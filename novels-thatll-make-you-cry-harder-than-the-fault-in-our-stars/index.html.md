# 7 Books That Will Make You Cry Harder Than The Fault in Our Stars

John Green has the remarkable power to make readers of every generation shed genuine tears. However, there are plenty of other authors who are just as skilled at crafting unforgettable tear-jerkers. If you are searching for books that will make you cry or novels like The Fault in Our Stars that hit you right in the heart, you are in the right place. Some of these titles are world-famous bestsellers, while others are hidden gems waiting to be discovered. If you enjoy emotional reads that should come with a box of tissues, here is the ultimate reading list to add to your bookshelf.

| Book Title | Core Theme | Emotional Vibe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My Sister's Keeper | Family Sacrifice & Illness | Deeply Moving |
| Tuesdays with Morrie | Life Lessons & Grief | Bittersweet & Inspiring |
| Wonder | Resilience & Kindness | Heartwarming Tear-Jerker |

## 1. My Sister's Keeper by Jodi Picoult

![My Sister's Keeper by Jodi Picoult](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/nc/hq/l42brier55383ef548968611650125_600x920.jpg)

You've probably heard of this iconic story by [Jodi Picoult](https://www.jodipicoult.com/my-sisters-keeper.html), especially since it was adapted into a major motion picture starring Cameron Diaz. The story centers on a young girl named Anna who was conceived specifically to serve as a medical donor for her older sister battling leukemia. Whenever a narrative revolves around childhood illness and ethical dilemmas, you know it will not be an easy read, but the emotional depth makes every single page worth it.

- Explores complex family dynamics and moral choices.
- Keeps you guessing with unexpected emotional twists.

## 2. Forbidden by Tabitha Suzuma

![Forbidden by Tabitha Suzuma](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/mg/v5/vfcpz4gp55383f6c3c3d4040550464_600x920.jpg)

This novel may prove challenging to read for more reasons than one. It tells the story of two teenage siblings who unexpectedly fall in love despite knowing their romantic feelings break every societal taboo. While romantic books that make you cry often focus on tragic circumstances, this story tackles a controversial boundary, offering a uniquely heartbreaking look at desperation and unconditional affection.

## 3. Tuesdays with Morrie by Mitch Albom

![Tuesdays with Morrie by Mitch Albom](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/aw/kz/x5l0fq0i553841cf60f16442809233_487x700.jpg)

This celebrated memoir by [Mitch Albom](https://www.mitchalbom.com/books/tuesdays-with-morrie/) recounts a man reconnecting with his former college professor during the professor's final months of life. Even as Morrie battles a terminal illness, he openly shares priceless wisdom about life, love, forgiveness, and coping with inevitable loss. For readers seeking Tuesdays with Morrie similar books, the gentle philosophy and profound emotional honesty will stay with you long after the final chapter.

## 4. Before I Fall by Lauren Oliver

![Before I Fall by Lauren Oliver](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/gf/j0/uc0cozqm553841fd7d1e5577099203_600x907.jpg)

This compelling YA novel follows a high school girl who tragically dies in a car accident, only to awaken the next morning to relive the final day of her life. Trapped in a time loop for seven days, she begins to understand how subtle choices alter outcomes for everyone around her. Much like the intense personal stakes in [My Sister's Keeper](#1), this story offers a powerful reflection on redemption, empathy, and making every moment count.

## 5. Wonder by R.J. Palacio

![Wonder by R.J. Palacio](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/dh/en/p24z5g455538421144ab7509795118_600x923.jpg)

If stories dealing with compassion and overcoming hardship move you, [Wonder](https://wonderthebook.com/) will deeply affect you. The narrative focuses on Auggie Pullman, a young boy with facial differences entering a mainstream school for the very first time. Flipping through these pages reveals how Auggie navigates peer acceptance, showing that kindness can overcome cruelty in the most uplifting way.

- The transformative power of empathy and friendship.
- Overcoming prejudice and embracing individuality.

## 6. The Five People You Meet in Heaven by Mitch Albom

![The Five People You Meet in Heaven by Mitch Albom](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/r7/nf/zpsnzxb255384227624e2876557837_600x870.jpg)

Have you ever pondered the purpose of your life journey? This novel explores that exact premise through Eddie, an elderly maintenance worker who dies saving a young girl. Upon entering the afterlife, he meets five individuals—some familiar, some strangers—who illuminate the hidden connections of his life. If you loved the reflection on mortality in [Tuesdays with Morrie](#3), this poignant story will give you a fresh perspective on human connection.

## 7. Marley and Me by John Grogan

![Marley and Me by John Grogan](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/jk/kd/s49ja1sd5538424aca8b6475993704_600x906.jpg)

If you have recently lost a beloved pet, read this heartwarming yet bittersweet memoir with caution. It details the hilarious and touching journey of raising a high-spirited Labrador named Marley. While the story is famous for bringing readers to tears, it is equally a celebration of unconditional love and loyalty. Just as readers connected with the raw heart in [Wonder](#5), anyone who has ever loved an animal will find Marley's journey deeply resonant.

If you are in the mood for books that will make you cry, pick up one of these classic recommendations and prepare yourself for an emotional roller coaster. Each of these stories brings a unique perspective on love, loss, and resilience. What tear-jerker novels have left a lasting mark on you?
