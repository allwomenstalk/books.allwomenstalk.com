# 7 Unforgettable Fault in Our Stars Quotes That Will Stay with You Forever

With John Green's masterpiece remaining a cornerstone of contemporary young adult literature, returning to _The Fault in Our Stars_ reminds us why these iconic quotes hit so deeply. Most of the famous lines spoken by Hazel Grace Lancaster and Augustus Waters in _The Fault in Our Stars_ are completely relatable, emotionally raw, and thought-provoking. They grab your attention, force you to reflect on life's deeper meanings, and linger in your mind long after turning the final page. The romance described in this book is tragic, witty, and atypical—it isn't your everyday teenage love story. It balances profound grief with razor-sharp humor. If you're exploring [reasons why you're never too old for young adult fiction](https://books.allwomenstalk.com/reasons-why-youre-never-too-old-for-young-adult-fiction/), John Green's writing is proof of the genre's emotional power. Here are seven memorable TFIOS quotes that will stay embedded in your heart and mind forever.

## 1. People Who Hurt You

‘You don’t get to choose if you get hurt in this world… but you do have some say in who hurts you. I like my choices.’ This quote states a fundamental truth about human vulnerability. Although pain and heartbreak are inevitable in life, you still maintain agency over who you let into your inner circle. You decide whom you surround yourself with and to whom you grant the power to touch your soul. Augustus Waters expresses that if he must suffer, he is deeply grateful that it stems from loving Hazel. This is one of the most famous Augustus Waters quotes in _The Fault in Our Stars_, capturing the beauty of conscious emotional risk.

- **Speaker:** Augustus Waters
- **Core Theme:** Emotional vulnerability and choice
- **Key Takeaway:** Love is worth the inevitable pain

## 2. My Thoughts

‘My thoughts are stars I cannot fathom into constellations.’ What makes Augustus such a compelling protagonist is his remarkable ability to express complex internal states, even when he feels unable to articulate them clearly. This quote beautifully describes those overwhelming moments when our minds overflow with ideas, feelings, and chaotic thoughts that defy logical organization. It highlights why fans frequently look for these poignant [expressive quotes](https://books.allwomenstalk.com/expressive-quotes-from-jane-austen/) across classic and modern literature. It connects readers directly to the characters' inner worlds.

## 3. You Gave Me a Forever

‘I cannot tell you how thankful I am for our little infinity. I wouldn’t trade it for the world. You gave me forever within the numbered days, and I’m grateful.’ This emotional climax is one of the quotes from _The Fault in Our Stars_ that brings readers to tears every time. Hazel's eulogy for Augustus packs immense emotional resonance into a few short sentences. It reminds us that the value of love isn't measured by duration, but by depth. ## 4. Reading a Book

‘Sometimes, you read a book and it fills you with this weird evangelical zeal, and you become convinced that the shattered world will never be put back together unless and until all living humans read the book.’ Hazel and Augustus form their deep bond through sharing their favorite novels, particularly _An Imperial Affliction_. Reading allows them to exchange intimate pieces of their souls. This quote perfectly captures the intense, transformative experience of discovering a book that reshapes your perspective. If you are constantly building your reading list and searching for [books that should be turned into movies](https://books.allwomenstalk.com/books-that-should-be-turned-into-movies/), you know that exact feeling of wanting the entire world to experience a literary masterpiece. Readers can also browse reader reviews and community discussions on [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/11870085-the-fault-in-our-stars).

## 5. Falling in Love

‘As he read, I fell in love the way you fall asleep: slowly, and then all at once.’ This line is widely regarded as one of the most romantic quotes in modern fiction. It captures the quiet, almost imperceptible way affection builds until it completely encompasses your heart. At first we resist emotional attachment out of fear, but eventually, love takes over entirely. It is a rare and precious experience that stays with us forever.

## 6. I Am a Grenade

‘I’m a grenade and at some point I’m going to blow up and I would like to minimize the casualties, okay?’ This quote offers essential insight into Hazel Grace's psychological mindset. Fearing her illness will cause unbearable grief to those who love her, she tries to distance herself from others to prevent collateral damage. Hazel's grenade quote highlights her selfless desire to protect Augustus and her parents, even at the cost of her own solitude. Much like Hazel's journey to embrace [falling in love](#5), understanding her fear of hurting others makes her emotional growth all the more inspiring.

## 7. The World

‘The world is not a wish granting factory.’ This recurring motif throughout the novel grounds the narrative in sober reality. While life grants occasional moments of magic, it does not guarantee happy endings or unfair plot twists. Accepting this truth allows Hazel and Augustus to cherish their limited time together without false illusions. It is a touching reminder that life's fragility makes every shared moment immeasurably precious.

- **Key Takeaway:** Life is unpredictable and unvarnished
- **Emotional Lesson:** Treasure every numbered day with loved ones
- **Overall Impact:** A timeless message of hope amid heartbreak

_The Fault in Our Stars_ continues to touch readers around the globe with its humor, honesty, and emotional vulnerability. While it is a fictional story, it offers timeless lessons on love, mortality, and the priceless value of our numbered days. It is a profoundly moving book that everyone should experience at least once.
